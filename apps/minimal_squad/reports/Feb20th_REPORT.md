# CEPE-style Experiment Results: ModernBERT Encoder + TinyLlama Decoder

## Setup

All experiments use `C/Q//A` format (context+question in encoder, answer generated by decoder) with TinyLlama 1B as the pretrained decoder and cross-attention adapters.

## Results Summary

| Experiment | Encoder | What's Trained | Trainable | Best EM% | Best F1% | Final EM% | Final F1% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `400m_frozen` | 400M | Adapters only | 371M (20%) | 43.4 (ep4) | 56.1 (ep4) | 41.6 | 53.9 |
| `150m_frozen` | 150M | Adapters only | 371M (23%) | 22.4 (ep5) | 31.9 (ep5) | 22.4 | 31.9 |
| `400m_cepe` | 400M | Adapters + Encoder | 766M (41%) | **77.6 (ep3)** | **85.4 (ep3)** | 76.4 | 85.0 |
| `150m_cepe` | 150M | Adapters + Encoder | 520M (32%) | 55.6 (ep5) | 70.1 (ep3) | 55.6 | 69.4 |
| `400m_finetune` | 400M | Everything | 1,866M (100%) | 78.4 (ep3) | 86.6 (ep3) | 76.6 | 85.2 |

## Key Findings

### 1. CEPE matches full fine-tuning with half the trainable params

`400m_cepe` (77.6% EM, 766M trainable) essentially ties `400m_finetune` (78.4% EM, 1,866M trainable). Fine-tuning the encoder while keeping the decoder frozen is sufficient — the decoder's pretrained language modeling capability doesn't need task-specific adaptation.

### 2. Adapters alone are not enough

`400m_frozen` (43.4% EM) is ~35 points behind CEPE (77.6% EM). The cross-attention adapters can bridge encoder and decoder, but without encoder fine-tuning the encoder representations aren't task-adapted enough for strong performance.

### 3. Encoder size matters significantly

Across both freeze strategies, 400M consistently outperforms 150M:
- Frozen: 43.4 vs 22.4 (+21 EM)
- CEPE: 77.6 vs 55.6 (+22 EM)

The gap stays roughly constant, suggesting encoder capacity is a bottleneck regardless of training strategy.

### 4. Overfitting appears at epoch 3-4

Both `400m_cepe` and `400m_finetune` peak at epoch 3, then degrade. The 150M experiments are still improving at epoch 5, suggesting smaller models need more training.

## Takeaway

The CEPE strategy (`--pretrained_weight_updating 0.0 --encoder_weight_updating 0.333`) is the recommended default for pretrained decoder experiments: it matches full fine-tuning quality while training only 41% of parameters and preserving decoder weights.
